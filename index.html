Professional profile

Scientific software, laboratory systems and validation-oriented documentation

My background combines biotechnology education, analytical chemistry, STEM mentoring, technical writing for laboratory equipment and software development for scientific workflows. The current focus is software for UV-Vis instruments, laboratory data flows and reproducible research prototypes.

In projects I describe the measurement process, data model, user interface states, acceptance criteria, test evidence and operating documentation. Public repositories are presented with their maturity level and known limitations.

Engineering cases

Research prototypes and instrument-software work

This section separates public research prototypes from private instrument-software cases. Public repositories include documentation, architecture notes and stated limitations.

01 Public research alpha

Q2SC Platform

Research alpha prototype for computational chemistry and predictive spectroscopy. It combines molecule preparation, PySCF-based quantum calculations, QSAR/QSPR, chemometrics, traceability notes and a React/Electron interface. Models and spectral profiles require separate scientific validation.

  • FastAPI
  • React
  • QSAR

02 Public prototype

BioCult-KB

Digital-bioreactor research prototype for modelling Aspergillus sydowii cultivation. The repository includes a structured knowledge base, FastAPI web app, process simulation, GEM-lite COBRA/SBML model, OMICS resources and operating documentation.
