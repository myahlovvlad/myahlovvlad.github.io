OPERATING MODEL

Translate implicit domain knowledge into explicit system behaviour.

That pattern appears repeatedly: a laboratory method becomes a workflow; a screen mockup becomes an FSM; a serial protocol becomes a HAL and state model; a field anomaly becomes a reproducible test and CAPA loop.

01

Understand the physical system

Measurements, optics, controls, operator sequence and failure modes define the real problem.

02

Build explicit contracts

States, commands, transitions, schemas and acceptance criteria reduce interpretation at system boundaries.

03

Close the loop with evidence

Real hardware, logs, plots and retests decide whether the engineering model matches reality.
